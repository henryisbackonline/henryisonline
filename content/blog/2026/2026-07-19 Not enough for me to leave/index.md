In a [recent post](../enough-is-enough), I lamented about the loss of Eleventy and the transition to Build Awesome and my dissatisfaction with the whole ordeal. Looking back, I got *way* to far over my skis about it.

Truthfully, I bet the house on Eleventy when I built this website. If I wasn't the way that I am (and was a lot smarter), I would have used a tool like [Bear Blog](https://bearblog.dev) or [Pika](https://pika.page) — and in the case of Pika, I actually did use it and still recommend it. However, because of the way that I am, I wanted nothing more than to make things difficult for myself and roll my own website. This is still how I feel. I've also reached a point with this whole setup where writing in Obsidian[^1], using a simple keyboard shortcut with an extension[^2], and then using my [shell script](../a-shell-script-to-make-my-life-easier) is just so much faster the faffing about with a web-based editor. I mean, it probably *isn't*, but I have a sunk-cost fallacy about it at this point.

Please hear the enormous sigh I give when I say this: I really don't know anymore. In some ways, I'm glad Eleventy has a business model that works, but in some ways I also just feel weird about the whole ordeal. This whole thing is a [project](https://github.com/merlinmann/wisdom/blob/master/wisdom.md) for me, and I love/hate/love tinkering with this website and its infrastructure about as much as I enjoy writing these posts for it, which is to say *I like it a lot.* I probably won't change anything. Or I might migrate to Bear Blog. Who knows. I'll be sticking with Eleventy for the foreseeable future at least, If only because it works so well now and I can't destroy the have the one truly functional[^3] technology stack I have on my life at the moment because I have feelings about Javascript. I *do not* have the time to create my own homework. 

[^1]: Obsidian might be one of two exceptions to my "No Electron apps" rule/preference. It's generally well behaved and I get a lot of use out of it. I just dislike the trend of websites becoming applications, but I might just be old man yelling at clouds at this point. 

[^2]: This is an Obsidian-specific thing that I have very mixed feelings about. Extensions aren't the right solution the the problems with Obsidian, and more people end up trying to turn it into some fucked-up variant of Notion when they should just use it to write things down. I have strong feeling about this. I'm probably yelling at clouds.

[^3]: Though I will say, the HTML that Eleventy currently spits out for my site looks *awful.* This is most likely user error, especially when so many other Eleventy-generated site on the Internet have excellent HTML. Some deranged part of me thinks that I could write better HTML by hand even though I get the markdown link brackets the wrong way around half the time. In fact, I *know* I couldn't write better HTML by hand because I wrote my CSS by hand and it looks even worse. I also know that I would feel this bizarre sense of pride if I hand-coded everything from scratch.
