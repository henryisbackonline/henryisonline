---

# **Nothing in this website will never be made with AI.**

Call it a policy if you like. I would call it "having integrity".

Frankly, I find the whole "AI" thing to be deeply insidious. I think it's a waste of money and energy, and we shouldn't encourage it's use. I might write about my feelings on "AI" more deeply at some point — and when I do, I'll link it here — but suffice it to say, I will never use it.
